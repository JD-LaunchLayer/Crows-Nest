About this version

UK time, including daylight saving. Physical activities occupy real minutes, with configurable estimates in crow-config.json. Quick research processes retrieved text without a simulated reading timer; Crow can also choose leisurely reading. Changes occur on completion.

Emotions are simulated state. Reflections, interests and tentative self-observations develop from completed experiences. Facts and interpretations are stored separately. Bodily needs arise from active time and effort; practical attempts retain predictions, measured results and tested methods. These are deliberately simplified simulation rules.

The library retrieves Wikipedia excerpts chosen by Crow. They are untrusted reference material, not instructions. Reading uses the internet; AI inference remains local. Crow has a limited toolset and cannot invent new tools.

Progress is in crow-save.json. Detailed events are archived in crow-events.jsonl and read articles in crow-reading. Closing or pausing stops activity progress; the real-world clock continues. Save backups are made on upgrade.
