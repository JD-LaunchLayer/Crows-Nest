# First causal learning loop — v0.5.0

Jordan supplies opportunities. Crow chooses intentions, methods, interpretations
and whether to continue. This milestone is a foundation to evaluate, not a
claim that human life or feelings have been reproduced.

## A small body with coupled processes

`life.py` keeps nourishment reserve, food being digested, hydration, sleep debt
and exertion. Numeric variables represent mechanisms; they are not happiness
rewards. Digestion transfers food into reserve gradually. Effort consumes
reserve/water and increases exertion; rest recovers exertion; sleep also reduces
sleep debt. The compatibility energy indicator is derived from these processes.
Perception exposes sensations and working impairment, not an instruction queue.
Crow can choose how to respond, including continuing a valued activity.

For auditability these are the authored estimates, not clinical physiology:

| Process | Rate per active hour |
| --- | --- |
| Digestion | Up to 18 internal nourishment units from stomach to reserve |
| Reserve expenditure | 2.5 + 5 × activity effort |
| Hydration expenditure | 2 + 4 × activity effort |
| Sleep debt | +0.65 awake; −1.8 asleep |
| Exertion | Effort-dependent increase; faster recovery during rest/sleep |
| Fruit renewal | 0.25 servings, capped at 12 |
| Spring recharge | 2 litres, capped at 40 |

Initial provisions are 24 ready-to-eat servings and 8 ripe fruit servings;
they are opportunities, not a cooking assignment. One meal moves up to one
serving into the stomach. Drinking is bounded by available water and capacity.
The pantry does not refill. Fruit and groundwater renewal are explicit world
rules, not a complete agricultural or fluid simulation. Removing an available
source from the world makes its use fail honestly. Trees retain their existing
finite stocks and do not gain automatic regrowth.

All processes advance during active simulation time, including local inference
work. Pause, maintenance and time with the program closed do not invent lived
activity, bodily deprivation, replenishment or emotional recovery. The visible
UK calendar still follows the actual PC clock. This model does not simulate
injury, disease or death.

## Predictions, attempts and observable consequences

The first measured domain is small wood carving. The supported primitive
shapes have a 140 mm nominal width. Before new work the agent records a
hypothesis, expected width error, predicted success, chosen tolerance and
working parameters: support, pass type and direction. These are agent choices;
there is no owner-set project. Legacy work with no prediction is not given one
retroactively.

A deterministic approximate execution model produces width deviation from the
method, relevant accumulated practice and current bodily fatigue. The result
is encoded into the actual saved part width. Height compensates so the piece
still contains its paid kilogram of wood. The engine measures the resulting
width against the target and stores the criterion result and prediction error.
A missed criterion is not a disappearing object: its rough workpiece remains
in the world. This is a narrow motor/task model, not finite-element wood cutting
or proof that an entire sculpture has the intended meaning or function.

The parameter model deliberately has simple discoverable relationships. It
should later be extended with more task-dependent trade-offs and validation;
we do not claim complex craftsmanship has already emerged from it.

## Reusable methods and transfer

The procedure ledger stores typed parameter combinations that Crow actually
tried, their attempts/successes, best measured result, shapes tested and source
event IDs. The agent can select a saved method ID for another supported shape.
Its underlying motor-control practice also transfers across those shapes.
Successful use on one shape does not certify success everywhere. Failed methods
remain marked as tested without success. Reading adds no motor-control time.

This is reusable parameterised procedure learning, not unrestricted new tool
creation, executable self-programming or model-weight training. Future domains
can use the same prediction/result/evidence interface with their own actual
world validators instead of claiming success from a generated description.

## Time, interruptions and continuity

Long physical sessions offer a continuation decision every 15 minutes. The
local model receives the current goal, remaining work, bodily signals and
context. It chooses to continue or suspend. Suspended work preserves its
original hypothesis, method, duration and remaining time. Choosing resume_work
restores that exact session; consuming food or resting does not finish it.
Other long-duration activities can gain richer interruption rules later.

Existing construction still uses bounded geometry/material accounting. The
new measured learning model does not certify building strength, cooking,
archery accuracy or universal practical skill. Legacy practice counters are
retained but not presented as measured accuracy improvements.

## Emotion as contextual appraisal

After an executor-recorded outcome the model can report what it means for the
concern that led to the attempt, with bounded valence, arousal and importance.
The engine attaches the actual event ID. Reactions are not selected by an
activity-to-emotion table. The same failed attempt can be appraised differently
because its meaning differs. Signals persist and decay according to active
elapsed time and their reported importance, towards a neutral zero contribution
rather than a compulsory cheerful baseline. Old emotion fields remain in saved
history for compatibility; the new policy uses current affect and evidence.

This does not validate human subjective experience. The language model can
misinterpret events; the journal labels its interpretation separately from
measurements. Future work should test appraisal quality with actual local model
runs and examine whether histories reliably change later decisions.

## Evidence and validation

Automated counterfactual checks cover matched bodies doing work versus resting,
fatigue versus rested performance, poor versus corrected methods, transfer to
another supported shape, prediction mismatch, material conservation, physical
width measurement, gradual digestion, finite/renewable supplies, no offline
advance, neutral versus consequential appraisal, and pause/resume/reload.
Existing simulation, research, world, mobile, maintenance and scene checks are
also required. UI tests use a small DOM and Three.js scene tests a stub renderer;
these do not replace actual browser/iPhone rendering or Qwen decision-quality
checks on Jordan's PC. No claim is made that an automated test proves emotion,
open-ended intelligence or research novelty.


## Research progress instead of repeated exposure

Source selection is assessed by the local model over actual Wikipedia candidate
titles/snippets, with an explicit no-relevant-result outcome. Selection is not a
guarantee of relevance. The agent sees prior failures, source counts and whether
its question was answered. Task research does not incur a leisure timer. Content
fingerprints distinguish a repeat excerpt from new evidence, so repeating an
unchanged source cannot repeatedly strengthen interests or enter fresh long-term
lessons. The full reading history and personal processing notes remain visible;
intentional rereading is still possible. This can reduce a loop without proving
that every local model run will escape repetition.
